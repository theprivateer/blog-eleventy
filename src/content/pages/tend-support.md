Tend is a simple recurring-task app for iPhone. Here are answers to the most common
questions. (This is a condensed version of the [in-depth FAQ](/tend-frequently-asked-questions))

## Common questions

**How do I add a task?**
Tap the **+** button in the bottom-right corner. You can also press and hold the Tend icon
on your Home Screen and choose **New Task**.

**How do I mark a task done?**
Tap the circle on the left of the task (there's a brief pause so you can cancel a mistap),
swipe right on it, or press and hold for the menu. Undo is always available on a completed
task.

**How do reminders work?**
Reminders are off by default. Open the **···** menu → **Settings**, turn on **Task
Reminders**, and choose a time. Tend will remind you on the day each task is due, at that
time. Reminders are delivered on your device only.

**Do my tasks sync to my other devices or to iCloud?**
No. Tend stores everything on the device you created it on, and has no cloud sync. A
standard iPhone backup will include the app's data as part of your device backup.

**What's the difference between archiving and deleting a task?**
Archiving hides a task but keeps it (and its history) so you can restore it later. Deleting
removes a task and its history permanently. If in doubt, archive.

**I completed a task but it still shows as due — why?**
Completing a task is what schedules the next occurrence. If it still shows due, it probably
wasn't marked complete (for example, the tap-to-complete was cancelled). Tap the circle to
complete it.
