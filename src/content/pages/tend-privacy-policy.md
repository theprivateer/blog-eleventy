_Last updated: 1 August 2026_

Tend is a personal task app for iPhone. This policy explains how Tend handles your
information. The short version: **Tend keeps everything on your device and collects
nothing.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Tend has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no servers. We do not collect,
receive, see, or store any of your information.

## Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in Tend — your tasks, groups, notes, and completion history — is
stored only on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device storage. It is never uploaded to us
or to anyone else, because Tend makes no network connections of its own.

## No tracking, analytics, or advertising

Tend contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any
kind. Tend does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not ask to track you across
other apps or websites.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders in Settings, Tend schedules local notifications on your device so
iOS can remind you when a task is due. These reminders are created and delivered entirely
on your iPhone — no reminder information is sent to us or over the internet. Reminders are
off until you choose to enable them, and you can turn them off at any time.

## Backups

If you back up your iPhone (through iCloud Backup or a computer), that backup is managed by
Apple and may include Tend's on-device data as part of your overall device backup. This is
Apple's backup mechanism, governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) — Tend itself does not send
your data anywhere.

## Children's privacy

Because Tend collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. Tend is suitable
for users of all ages.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update it on this page and revise the date above.
Because Tend collects nothing, we do not anticipate material changes.
