## Getting started

### What is Tend for?
Tend is a simple recurring-task tracker. It's built for the things you do
again and again — water the plants, pay a bill, change the filter — as well as
one-off to-dos. You tell it how often something recurs, and it keeps bringing
it back when it's due.

### How do I add a task?
Tap the **+** button in the bottom-right corner. You can also add a task
without opening the app first: press and hold the Tend icon on your Home
Screen and choose **New Task**.

### Do I need an account or to sign in?
No. Tend works straight out of the box with no account, no email, and no
sign-up. Your tasks live on your device.

## Everyday use

### How do I mark a task done?
Tap the circle on the left of the task. There's a brief one-second pause
before it's confirmed — tap again during that moment to cancel if it was a
mistake. You can also swipe right on a task to complete it, or use the
context menu (press and hold).

### I completed something by accident. Can I undo it?
Yes. Completed tasks show an undo arrow — tap it, or swipe right on the task
and choose **Undo**. This restores the previous due date and removes that
completion from the log.

### What's the difference between "Complete", "Skip", and "Nudge"?
- **Complete** records that you did it and schedules the next occurrence.
- **Skip** moves a recurring task to its next occurrence *without* recording
  that you did it — handy for a week you're deliberately skipping.
- **Nudge** pushes the task to tomorrow, leaving everything else unchanged.

### Can I add a note to a task?
Yes. Every task has an optional note field you can fill in when creating or
editing it. Tasks with a note show a small document icon in the list. You can
also attach a one-off note to a specific completion via **Complete with Note**.

### Can I get reminders when a task is due?
Yes. Reminders are **off by default** — to turn them on, open the ··· menu →
**Settings** and switch on **Task Reminders** (iOS will ask permission the first
time). Once on, Tend reminds you on the day each task is due. Reminders are
delivered **on this device only** — Tend has no servers — and, for privacy on
your lock screen, a reminder shows the task's title but never its note.

### Can I choose when reminders arrive?
Yes — there's a single reminder time in **Settings** (for example, 9am) that
applies to all your reminders, and Tend notifies you at that time on each task's
due day. You get one reminder per task on its due day; Tend won't keep nagging
you while a task is overdue.

### How do the recurring schedules work?
When you create a task you pick a frequency — daily, weekly, monthly,
quarterly, or yearly — and can repeat every *n* of those (e.g. every 2 weeks).
You can also choose whether the next due date is calculated from the original
due date or from when you actually completed it. That second option is useful
for things like "water the plant 3 days after I last did", where the clock
should start from completion rather than the calendar.

### What do the Today / 7 Days / 30 Days tabs mean?
They're just windows onto what's coming up: **Today** is anything due today or
overdue, **7 Days** covers the coming week, and **30 Days** the coming month.
There's also an **All Tasks** view (via the ··· menu) that shows everything
with no date filter.

### Can I group my tasks?
Yes. You can create groups (e.g. Home, Work, Garden) and assign tasks to them.
Tasks are organised by group by default. You can switch to sorting by date
instead from the ··· menu's **Sort By** option.

### Where do my completed tasks go?
Recurring tasks reappear at their next due date once completed. On the Today
view, things you've finished today collapse into a **Completed Today** section
at the bottom. There's also a **Completion Log** (in the ··· menu) showing your
recent completion history across all tasks.

### A one-off task disappeared after I completed it — is that a bug?
No, that's intended. One-off tasks don't recur, so once done they drop out of
your active lists to keep them tidy. You can still find them in the Completion
Log.

## Managing tasks

### What's the difference between archiving and deleting?
- **Archive** tucks a task away out of your active lists but keeps it (and its
  history). Archived tasks live in **Archived Tasks** in the ··· menu, and you
  can restore them any time. It's reversible.
- **Delete** removes a task and its completion history for good. This can't be
  undone.

If you're not sure, archive it.

### Can I edit a task after creating it?
Yes — tap a task to open it, then tap **Edit**. You can change its title,
note, frequency, group, and scheduling. Swiping left on a task also offers a
quick Edit action.

### Can I clear my completion history?
Yes. Open the **Completion Log** and use **Clear Log**. It asks you to confirm
first, because it can't be undone.

## What Tend doesn't do (yet)

We're keeping Tend deliberately simple. A few things people often expect that
it doesn't currently do. (Reminders *are* supported — see
"Can I get reminders when a task is due?" above.)

### Do my tasks sync across my devices / to iCloud?
No. Tasks are stored only on the device you created them on. There's currently
no iCloud sync, no cloud backup, and no way to move tasks to another phone.
(A standard iPhone backup will include the app's data as part of your device
backup.)

### Is there an iPad, Mac, web, or Apple Watch version?
Not yet — Tend is an iPhone app for now.

### Can I set a specific time of day for a task?
Not per task — tasks are scheduled by day, not by hour. You can, however, set a
single **reminder time** in Settings that applies to all your reminders (e.g.
"remind me at 9am on the day things are due"). There's no per-task "remind me at
3pm for this one".

### Can I share tasks or collaborate with someone else?
No. Tend is a personal, single-user app — there's no sharing, assigning, or
shared lists.

### Is there a search box?
Not currently. You can browse by group, date, or the All Tasks view, but there
isn't a keyword search yet.

### Can I add subtasks, priorities, tags, or attachments?
No. Tend keeps each task to a title, an optional note, a group, and a
schedule. There are no checklists/subtasks, priority levels, tags (beyond
groups), or file/photo attachments.

### Can I reorder tasks manually?
No. Ordering is automatic — by group then title, or by date, depending on your
sort setting. There's no drag-to-reorder.

## Troubleshooting

### I lost my phone / got a new one — can I get my tasks back?
Only if they were included in an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that you
restore onto the new device. Because Tend doesn't sync to the cloud on its own,
there's no way to recover tasks from Tend's side if there's no device backup.

### A task shows as overdue but I'm sure I did it — what happened?
Completing a task is what advances it. If it still shows overdue, it likely
wasn't marked complete (for example, the one-second confirmation was cancelled,
or the swipe didn't register). Tap the circle to complete it now.
